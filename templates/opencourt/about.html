🏀 What is OpenCourt?

OpenCourt is a college basketball analytics platform built to make advanced stats accessible to everyone — no paywall, no subscription, no catch. We pull data from every Division I men's basketball program and transform it into the kind of analysis that used to be locked behind expensive paywalls.

Whether you're a casual fan trying to understand why your team keeps losing close games, a bracket builder looking for upset picks, or a stats nerd who dreams in efficiency ratings — OpenCourt has something for you.

{# ═══════════════════════ THE FOUR FACTORS ═══════════════════════ #}

📐 The Four Factors

Our analysis is built on Dean Oliver's Four Factors of Basketball Success — the framework used by NBA front offices and top college programs to evaluate team performance. These four metrics explain roughly 90% of why teams win or lose.

{# Factor 1: Shooting #}
40%

Shooting

Measured by Effective Field Goal % (eFG%), which accounts for three-pointers being worth more than two-pointers.

eFG% = (FG + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA
{# Factor 2: Turnovers #}
25%

Turnovers

Measured by Turnover Ratio — turnovers committed per 100 possessions. Every turnover is a wasted scoring opportunity.

TOV% = Turnovers / Possessions × 100
{# Factor 3: Rebounding #}
20%

Rebounding

Measured by Offensive Rebound % (ORB%) — the percentage of available offensive rebounds a team grabs, creating second-chance points.

ORB% = Off Rebounds / (Off Rebounds + Opp Def Rebounds)
{# Factor 4: Free Throws #}
15%

Free Throws

Measured by Free Throw Rate (FTR) — free throw attempts relative to field goal attempts. Getting to the line is one of the most efficient ways to score.

FT Rate = FTA / FGA

Source: Dean Oliver, Basketball on Paper (2004). See also Basketball Reference.

{# ═══════════════════════ EFFICIENCY RATINGS ═══════════════════════ #}

⚡ Efficiency Ratings

Raw points per game can be misleading — a team that scores 85 points in a fast-paced game isn't necessarily better than one scoring 65 in a slower one. That's why we use tempo-independent efficiency ratings:

Metric What It Measures Elite Average Poor
Offensive Rating Points scored per 100 possessions 115+ 100–110 <100
Defensive Rating Points allowed per 100 possessions <95 95–105 105+
Net Rating Off Rating − Def Rating +15 0 to +10 Negative
Pace Possessions per 40 minutes ~68 avg · Style indicator, not quality
{# ═══════════════════════ PREDICTIONS ═══════════════════════ #}

🔮 Predictions

Our prediction model uses a machine learning classifier trained on historical game outcomes. The feature set includes efficiency differentials, Four Factors matchups, pace adjustments, and home court advantage.

Every upcoming game gets a predicted winner and a confidence score. After games are played, we track our accuracy on the Results page so you can see exactly how the model performs — no cherry-picking.

Predictions are for entertainment only

All predictions are probabilistic estimates based on season-level statistics. They do not account for injuries, suspensions, travel fatigue, or other game-day factors. Do not use predictions as the sole basis for any financial decisions.
