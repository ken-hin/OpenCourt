📐 Our Methodology

The Four Factors

We use Dean Oliver's Four Factors framework — the most predictive indicators of team success in basketball:

  • Shooting (40%) — Effective FG%
  • Turnovers (25%) — Turnover ratio per 100 possessions
  • Rebounding (20%) — Offensive rebound %
  • Free Throws (15%) — Free throw rate (FTA/FGA)

Efficiency Ratings

Offensive and defensive ratings measure points scored/allowed per 100 possessions. Unlike raw points, these are tempo-independent — making them ideal for cross-team comparison.

A strong offense rates 110+. A strong defense rates 95 or below.
